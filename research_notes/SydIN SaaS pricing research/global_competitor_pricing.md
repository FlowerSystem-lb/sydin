# Global Inventory SaaS Competitor Pricing (2025–2026) vs SydIN's $9 / $19 tiers

> **How these numbers were gathered (read this first).** Research date: **9 Oct 2026**. This session's network proxy blocked direct page fetches from every official pricing site tried (sortly.com, help.sortly.com, zoho.com, inflowinventory.com, odoo.com, katanamrp.com, cin7.com), plus erpresearch.com and web.archive.org. So the "official" figures below come from **search-engine extracts of the official pages** (indexed copies, retrieved with search restricted to the vendor's own domain), not from a live page load. Where the search index gave the snapshot's age, it is noted. Rows marked **(secondary)** come from third-party aggregators (Capterra, G2, review blogs) because no official figure was found. Treat every price as "seen in the index in 2026", and re-check the live page before quoting it to customers.

## Q1. Tiers per product: names, monthly and annual USD prices, item limits, seats, locations, barcode scanning, free plan, trial

### Takeaway
The cheap, mobile-first tools (Inventory Now, BoxHero, Loyverse add-on, Zoho Standard, Sortly Advanced) charge about **$5–$49/month** for their first paid tier. The "operations" tools (inFlow, Fishbowl, Katana, Cin7) start at **$129–$349/month**. Most SMB tools offer a free plan capped at about 20–100 items (or 50 orders/month for Zoho) and a 14-day trial. BoxHero gives 30 days.

### Inferences
- **Sortly's real annual price after year one** is about $39.20/mo for Advanced ($49 × 0.8), $119.20 for Ultra and $239.20 for Premium. The $24 / $74 / $149 figures are first-year promotional prices (calculated from Sortly's stated 20% renewal discount).
- **Per-item price, closest comparisons:**
  - SydIN Standard ($9 for 250 items) works out to about $0.036 per item per month.
  - Sortly Advanced costs $0.048–$0.098 per item ($24–49 for 500 items).
  - BoxHero Business costs about $0.018–$0.020 per item ($18–20 for 1,000 items).
  - SydIN Pro ($19 for 1,000 items) is about $0.019 per item, the same as BoxHero Business. SydIN Pro allows 10 depots against BoxHero's 3 locations and 1 location on Zoho Free.
- **Free plans:** SydIN's Free plan (50 items, 1 depot) is smaller than Sortly Free and BoxHero Personal (100 items each) but larger than Katana Free (30 SKUs) and Inventory Now Free (20 items). Odoo One App Free (Inventory app, unlimited users) and Stockpile (unlimited) are free with no item cap at all.
- **Odoo in Lebanon** is probably the strongest "cheap full-suite" rival. The Inventory app alone is free with unlimited users. Adding a second app (for example Barcode, Sales or Purchase, if they are separate apps on the buyer's version) costs about $7.25–$8.95 per user per month on yearly billing, so a 2-user shop would pay about $15–$18/month. This sits just above SydIN Standard and close to SydIN Pro. Whether Barcode counts as a separate app that triggers Standard pricing was **not verified**.

## Q2. Which products publish lower prices for emerging markets / use purchasing-power pricing?

### Takeaway
Of the products checked, only **Odoo** clearly uses purchasing-power-style regional pricelists, and **Lebanon is reportedly on Odoo's cheapest "Middle East" USD pricelist**: about $7.25–$8.95 per user per month, against $24.90–$31.10 in the US. Zoho localizes prices by country site and currency, but no India, UAE, Egypt or Lebanon price for Zoho *Inventory* could be retrieved. The other vendors (Sortly, BoxHero, Katana, Cin7, inFlow, Lightspeed) show single USD or currency-converted lists with no emerging-market discount found.

### Inferences
- In Lebanon, Odoo is the one global brand already priced for the market. At about $9/user, a 1–2 user Odoo Standard shop pays roughly what SydIN Standard/Pro would cost. ## Q4. Which features do competitors gate behind higher tiers (barcode scanning, multiple locations, purchase orders, seats)?

### Takeaway
**Barcode *scanning* is usually included** in every tier, including free (Sortly states this explicitly). What gets gated is **barcode label printing** (Sortly), **barcode generation** (Zoho, at Premium) and **scanning apps for warehouse teams** (inFlow Stockroom sold separately; Katana/Cin7 warehouse modules). **Locations, seats and purchase orders are the most common upsell levers.** Free tiers get 1 location and 1 user, POs usually start at the second tier or a paid add-on, and transfers between locations often sit at the top tier.
